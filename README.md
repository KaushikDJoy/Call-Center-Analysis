📊 Project Description
The Call Center Performance Analysis project is an interactive, enterprise-grade Business Intelligence solution developed in Power BI to evaluate customer service operations. Utilizing an optimized star-schema data model, this project integrates complex transactional call logs with data models for agents, departments, time periods, and spatial geography. The primary objective of the dashboard is to transform raw call metrics into an intuitive visual platform, enabling management to monitor operational capacity, benchmark employee productivity, and ensure overall alignment with service workflows.

⚠️ Problem Findings

SLA Threshold Breaches: Transactional data logs revealed significant bottlenecks in the Average Speed of Answer (ASA), with prolonged queue wait times causing widespread failure to meet organizational Service Level Agreements (SLAs).

Workload Disparities & Burnout: Cross-filtering agent profiles against call volumes highlighted structural inefficiencies, where certain agent tiers handled disproportionately higher workloads while experiencing lower positive resolution rates.

High-Friction Call Drivers: Granular topic segmentation isolated frequent, localized spikes in customer inquiries regarding specific categories—such as Billing Inquiries and Delivery/Logistics—indicating major downstream friction points in service execution.

Regional Customer Dissatisfaction: Spatial and sentiment analysis mapped across regional territories exposed a clear correlation between delayed hold times and failing net Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) scores in specific geographical boundaries.

💡 Solutions 

ImplementedReal-Time SLA Target Monitoring: Developed a unified operational compliance view that flags delayed speed-of-answer intervals. This allows shift supervisors to quickly identify queue overloads and reallocate active lines dynamically.

Data-Driven Workforce Optimization: Created agent performance benchmarking layouts that plot Average Handling Time (AHT) against resolution success. This enables HR and team leads to adjust shift schedules to peak call hours and uncover training opportunities.

Root-Cause Friction Mitigation: Implemented automated topic hierarchy tracking to isolate problem categories. This equips department heads to resolve underlying systemic bugs in billing systems or delivery workflows before they overload the call center.

Geographical Quality Mapping: Integrated custom structural shape-maps to track localized satisfaction indices dynamically. This ensures senior executives can easily target regional support networks that require localized strategic intervention or infrastructure upgrades.
